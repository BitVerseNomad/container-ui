# Market and product brief (2026-10-23)

Sources: two research passes (GitHub issues on abiosoft/colima, Hacker News threads, vendor pages), the 2026-10-11 landscape note, and a check of one real Mac right after Docker Desktop was removed. Prices and star counts were read from vendor and GitHub pages on 2026-10-23; recheck them before quoting.

## Who this is for
Engineers whose company dropped Docker Desktop. They know `docker run` and `docker compose up`. Most do not know what a VM, a context or a CLI plugin is, and they should not have to.

## The market
1. **Docker Desktop** is free only for companies under 250 staff and under $10M revenue. Paid seats are Pro $11, Team $16 and Business $24 per user per month.
2. **OrbStack** is the polished option (fast, local domains, automatic HTTPS) but is paid for commercial use.
3. **Rancher Desktop and Podman Desktop** bring their own runtime, so they replace Colima rather than sit on top of it.
4. **Colima GUIs** are many and young: MacColi (menu bar), mx0r/colima-desktop (Compose grouping, port links, shell), vnknowledge2014/colima-ui (AI diagnostics, terminal), ColimaStack, nookat, colimator, oriel. None has more than about 60 stars. None explains problems in plain words.

## What breaks for people (ranked by how often it shows up)
1. **The VM won't start or gets stuck**: about 10 of the 25 most-discussed Colima issues (ssh "essential requirement", fd_connect, SHA sum errors). Stale `ha.sock` / `ha.pid` files after sleep; the fix is `colima stop --force`.
2. **Docker Desktop leftovers** (seen on the test Mac, all at once):
   1. 13 dead `~/.docker/cli-plugins` links into `/Applications/Docker.app`, so `docker compose` fails even with Homebrew's compose installed
   2. `cliPluginsExtraDirs` not set, so Docker never looks in Homebrew's plugin folder
   3. buildx not installed
   4. a leftover `desktop-linux` context
   5. no autostart (`brew services` reports colima as "none")
   6. the default 2 CPU, 2 GiB VM
   7. `credsStore: desktop` with its helper gone, which breaks pulls and logins (seen in issues; not on this Mac)
   8. Testcontainers pinned to Docker Desktop's socket
3. **Networking and DNS**, often under a VPN.
4. **Mounts outside $HOME**, and file permissions.
5. **x86 images** without Rosetta.
6. **Disk that only grows** (sparse disk, no shrink).

## Where we win
Everyone else lists containers. We say why something broke and fix it with one click, after showing the exact command. The connection doctor and the plain problem lines already do this. The Setup check now does it for the migration itself, which no competitor covers.

## Ranked roadmap
Ordered by how many users each item unblocks, using the research above and the 2026-10-23 product, design and engineering reviews.

1. **Setup check** (built 2026-10-23): finds and fixes Docker Desktop leftovers, with a strip on home while anything blocks.
2. **Start failures in plain words**: map the common colima and lima errors to one sentence and one next step, offer `colima stop --force` for stale lock files, and add "Copy diagnostics".
3. **Compose projects**: group containers by `com.docker.compose.project`, then up, down, restart and logs per project. Install compose and buildx during onboarding.
4. **Stopped and first-run states**: size the first VM from the Mac, open the settings form from "Customize first", and add a Start button wherever "isn't running" appears.
5. **Menu bar item and notifications**: VM state, problem count, Start/Stop; a notification when a container turns unhealthy or runs out of memory.
6. **Cleanup**: search, sort, bulk remove, and "reclaim N GB" on Images and Volumes.
7. **Native window frame**: overlay title bar with the sidebar under the traffic lights.
8. **VM settings**: vz, virtiofs, Rosetta, DNS, mounts outside $HOME.
